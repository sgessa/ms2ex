    Ms2ex.Context.Fishing (MS2EX v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Pure fishing behaviour: water-tile reachability, fish selection, bite timers and catch rolls, plus the session-map transitions.

    Stateless by design — the session and album state live in Ms2ex.Managers.Fishing, which turns these results into packets and persistence.

    The fish that can bite on the given tile, given the active lure.

    bite(fishing, tile, fish_id, fight_game?, bait_used?, bait)

    Drops the line: stores the tile, fish and bait state for the cast.

    bite_timer(rod, fish, auto_fishing?, bait)

    The bite delay and whether the catch turns into a fight minigame: a bite lands inside the bore window, a miss runs past it so the client times out.

    clear_minigame(fishing)

    The client lost the fight minigame; the bite stays but the game ends.

    fishing_lure?(arg1)

    True when the item's metadata marks a fishing lure.

    guide_position(tiles, character_position)

    The bobber position: one block above the water surface, on the reachable tile closest to the player.

    move_guide(fishing, position, rotation)

    Tracks where the player dragged the bobber.

    pick_weighted(fishes)

    Picks the fish that bites, weighted by the box weights.

    reachable_tiles(character)

    The water tiles the player can fish into: the client only lets a player fish into the quadrant they face, so the box in front of them is scanned for surface water.

    record_catch(album, fish_id, size, prize?)

    Records a catch in the album. Returns the updated album, the entry and whether this was the first catch of that kind.

    roll_size(fish)

    Indexes the reachable tiles by their block cell.

    The indexed tile at the given position's block cell, if any.
