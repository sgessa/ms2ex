    Ms2ex.Managers.Shop (MS2EX v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Per-character vendor shop state: the open shop window, its instanced stock for limited-stock shops, and the session's buy-back list.

    Unlimited stock is rebuilt from metadata on every open. Shops with a restock policy (enable_reset) keep their rolled stock and per-item purchase counters until the restock window passes; that state persists per owner (account or character, per the shop's policy) through Ms2ex.Context.Shops.
