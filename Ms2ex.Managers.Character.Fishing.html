    Ms2ex.Managers.Character.Fishing (MS2EX v0.1.0)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Fishing state owned by the character process: the active rod, the water tiles it reaches, the fish currently biting and the fish album.

    The album is persisted alongside the mastery state on the periodic flush.
